Equipment Accountability & Operating Guidelines

  • Read-Only Lookup Clearance: Employees have secure lookup permissions to trace equipment availability without risking accidental database overwrites.
  • Requesting Approved Exports: To generate audit reports or obtain full spreadsheets, submit a request via the Request Copy button. Once approved by Admin, download the CSV directly from your sidebar history.
  • Reporting Equipment Condition: If any municipal property requires maintenance or servicing, notify the GSO custodian promptly with the corresponding Property No.
